# Unguided sweep (night of Oct 1–2 2026)

The question: without shape ideas from anyone, can the search find good routes on its own? 24 seed tours were
generated mechanically by `scripts/random_seeds.py` (random start state, noisy nearest-unvisited walk over state
centres, straightened with 2-opt so each fits the 20-day timetable window). Each got one `time_search` run: free
start, **no fixed end**, about 13 min at 16 workers (most stopped earlier on exhaustion or patience). One run at a
time, detached, resumable (`out/sweep/queue.sh`; logs and the auto-generated ranked table are in `out/sweep/`,
gitignored).

Record 238.83 h, control 237.24 h, Wheeling 234.24 h.

**New Orleans is a basin, not a fluke:** three different seeds (begun in SD and two others) were each moved by the
search to the same New Orleans Thu 05:05 start and the same opening (Louisiana, Mississippi, Florida, then New
England). Independent convergence like that is the best evidence a sweep can give that a shape is real.

## What it says

- **Unguided search gets close fast.** From seeds evaluating at 285–377 h, the best runs reached 240.0 and 243.6 h in
  about 13 minutes each, within 1.2 and 4.8 h of the record, with no start or end given.
- **It does not just rediscover the known families.** None of the top three is a control or X shape:
  - **240.0, St George → Sheridan** is a reversed X: Southwest, South and Florida, New England, then back west through
    Kentucky, Michigan, North Dakota, Kansas and Washington, ending on JL 702 into Sheridan. HANDOFF 22 records
    hand-built reversals of X at 265–297 h, so this is a much better version of that family than anything tried.
  - **243.6, New Orleans → Louisville** is new.
  - **250.1, Needles → West Wendover** ends on the Wendover Fun Bus added the same night.
- **Where the endpoints land:** the runs end at sparse anchors over and over (Sheridan, Cheyenne, Missoula, Barstow,
  Mt Shasta, Covington, West Wendover), which is the same pattern the record routes show: put the sparse states at
  the ends.

**Missoula -> Omaha is really Sheridan -> Sioux City (239.17 h).** The Spearfish 18:45 is Jefferson's I-90 bus, which
also stops at Sheridan at 22:20, and South Dakota comes later anyway (Fargo -> Sioux Falls), so boarding at Sheridan
costs nothing: 240.33 h on the same order. Nick then had the finish leave the Jefferson bus at Sioux City (Sun 22:00)
and walk the Veterans Memorial Bridge into Nebraska (30 min, `walk_links.csv`), not ride on to Omaha (23:40):
**239.17 h**, all 48, 0.34 h off the record before any focused search. Getting that to count found a router bug:
the guard against collecting a state by walking into a real stop also blocked every hand-measured walk link's
end point, and coverage ignored them, so no measured border walk had ever collected its state. Fixed with
`TimeRouter.walk_collects`; `regress.py` has a check that fails without it. A focused Sheridan -> Nebraska search
is queued after sweep 2.

Nick's rule of thumb, which these fit: a sweep shape needs to land near 245 h or better to be optimisable into a
record. RX started at 240; the families that started above 250 moved 0 to 3 h, except Missoula -> Omaha, which
changed shape (its start moved to Spearfish) rather than being tuned.

What it says:

- **Two basins found by both sweeps from unrelated seeds:** Saint George -> Sheridan (RX's family, 240.0 both times)
  and New Orleans (243.6 both times). Independent convergence is the strongest evidence a sweep gives.
- **A new spine shared by runs 07, 13 and 15:** Minneapolis -> Fargo -> Sioux Falls -> (Nebraska in the dead time) ->
  the 12:40 I-90 bus to Billings -> Spokane -> Pasco -> Salt Lake -> Las Vegas -> San Bernardino -> Southwest Chief
  to Newton -> BeeLine to Oklahoma City -> Dallas / Houston -> Tallahassee. The Sioux Falls -> Maryville -> Sioux Falls
  detour all three make costs nothing: it fills the wait for the once-daily I-90 bus (measured: Billings Tue 00:25
  with or without Nebraska). Taking the I-90 bus straight from Minneapolis instead and fetching North Dakota and
  Nebraska later costs at least 11.5 h (run 13: 239.8 -> 251.3 best), so the Fargo loop is the cheapest North Dakota.
- **Fargo is a start basin** for these seeds (runs 03, 04, 09, 10, 11, 19), all at 245-254 h.

Focused searches queued after it (`out/focus2/after_sweep2.sh`), one at a time: Nick's I-80 Wyoming shape (Primm ->

103.5 h, a day faster than without the Cheyenne chain), Sheridan -> Nebraska (239.17), runs 13, 07 and 15.
