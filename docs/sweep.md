# Unguided sweep (night of Oct 1–2 2026)

The question: without shape ideas from anyone, can the search find good routes on its own? 24 seed tours were
generated mechanically by `scripts/random_seeds.py` (random start state, noisy nearest-unvisited walk over state
centres, straightened with 2-opt so each fits the 20-day timetable window). Each got one `time_search` run: free
start, **no fixed end**, about 13 min at 16 workers (most stopped earlier on exhaustion or patience). One run at a
time, detached, resumable (`out/sweep/queue.sh`; logs and the auto-generated ranked table are in `out/sweep/`,
gitignored).

Record 238.83 h, control 237.24 h, Wheeling 234.24 h.

## What it says

- **Unguided search gets close fast.** From seeds evaluating at 285–377 h, the best runs reached 240.0 and 243.6 h in
  about 13 minutes each, within 1.2 and 4.8 h of the record, with no start or end given.
- **It does not just rediscover the known families.** None of the top three is a control or X shape:
  - **240.0, St George → Sheridan** is a reversed X: Southwest, South and Florida, New England, then back west through
    Kentucky, Michigan, North Dakota, Kansas and Washington, ending on JL 702 into Sheridan. HANDOFF 22 records
    hand-built reversals of X at 265–297 h, so this is a much better version of that family than anything tried.
  - **243.6, New Orleans → Louisville** is new.
  - **250.1, Needles → West Wendover** ends on the Wendover Fun Bus added the same night.
- **Where the endpoints land:** the runs end at sparse anchors over and over (Sheridan, Cheyenne, Missoula, Barstow,
  Mt Shasta, Covington, West Wendover), which is the same pattern the record routes show: put the sparse states at
  the ends.

Two defects found by these runs, both in how focused searches start, and the reason the first focused pass looked
flat:

- **Free start re-settles from scratch.** `--start free:<state> --seed run.pkl` re-chose the start stop and began
  4-20 h worse than the sweep result (run 22: 240.5 -> 260.2), so a search spent its hour climbing back. Use
  `--start "<the result's START stop>" --seed run.pkl --seed-pins`, and check the log's `settled` equals the sweep
  FINAL.
- **The descent could leave a good seed** (fixed in d90d8f0): it ranks on finish from the fixed clock and settles
  the start day only afterwards, so run 22 came back 243.8 from a 240.5 seed. The settled seed is now kept as a
  candidate. With both fixes run 22 went 240.5 -> 237.52.

The HT / HAR / HMS focused searches of Oct 2 ran before both fixes; their families may have more in them.

Follow-ups (Oct 3): HT focused again with both fixes (start Ledyard Lot, settled at its own 230.0 h, free end):
converged at **230.02**, nothing better in an hour. NBA's ending re-timed with Arkansas and Mississippi at every
position: as found (237.52) is best; both are only reachable from Memphis at the end, any other placement costs

HT split at its once-daily I-90 bus (Sioux Falls Mon 12:40) and each half searched with the other fixed (Oct 3):
- **Tail** (fixed start Chicago Museum Campus Sat 17:19 with HT's 21 states done, free order and finish): 170.1 h,

- **Front** (free start, be at Sioux Falls by Mon 12:35 holding the 28 states HT has there): HT's own front, same

So HT is locally optimal on both sides of its key anchor under current data. A faster route needs a different
family: HT already has the least useful riding (~173 h once the Maryville loop is counted as waiting); its ~49 h of
waiting, half of it in the Plains before the I-90 bus, is where a better shape would have to save.
