# Attempt prep: what the rider has to do on foot

The routes in `routes.md` are timetable results. This file is the other half: what they ask of the person riding
them. Add to it whenever a route or variant leans on the rider rather than on a carrier.

## Training

**Train for a 10K, and be able to hold about 8 mph (a 7:20 mile) for an hour.** No planned route needs it, but the
best optional variant does (the South Bend run below), and the same fitness is what rescues a missed transfer
anywhere a stop is a mile or two from the next one. Ten days of sitting on buses will cost some of it, so arrive
with margin.

## Optional runs

**RX, South Bend to the South Shore Line (Wednesday).** Off the 15:52 Blackthorn at Adams & Dylan, about a mile on
Mayflower to the Michigan line, then about 6.5 miles to South Bend Airport for the South Shore 16:52 (Chicago
17:55 CT). 7.5 miles in 60 minutes, about 8.2 mph with five minutes to board. It saves no time on the record (the
22:00 Greyhound out of Chicago absorbs it); it trades 1.5 h on a rural corner for about 4 h in Chicago to eat and
clean up. Miss it and the planned route is intact: 17:52 Blackthorn back, 18:55 Greyhound. A ride-hail also does
it. Details in `routes.md` under RX.
